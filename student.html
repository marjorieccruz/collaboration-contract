Your tools

1. Individual Collaboration Contract

Your own reflection, before the team agrees anything. Visible to you, to the teaching team and to the researchers. Your teammates do not see it.

2. Team Collaboration Contract

Filled in together with your group. Everyone in the group sees it, and it can be revisited later in the semester as a new round.

3. DISC Reflection

A self-reflection on working style. Runs on a separate, anonymous tool: the answers stay in your browser and never reach this platform. Can be repeated later in the semester.

4. Initial Team Reflection

A first look at how the team is working. Separate, anonymous tool.

5. Weekly Reflection Questionnaire

Repeated during the semester. Separate, anonymous tool.
