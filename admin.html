Add a coach or researcher

Creates the account and shows a temporary password once. Pass it on, and ask the person to change it later. Coaches see groups, contracts, individual contracts and their own session notes. Researchers see the same except the session notes, and cannot create groups.

What is in the database

Backups and exports

Session notes are never included. For a full backup of the database, use phpMyAdmin in your hosting panel.

Groups

A coach with no group assigned sees every group. As soon as you assign at least one group to a coach, that coach sees only the assigned ones, in the dashboard, in the individual contracts and in the activity feed. Researchers and the owner always see everything.

Clear a group's data

Deletes the team contract and its history, the research events, the individual contracts and the memberships of one group. Students who were in the group will have to enter the code again. This cannot be undone, so type DELETE to confirm.
