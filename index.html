:the idea

The collaboration contract

When a new group forms, most friction later comes from things never said at the start: what we each expect, how much time we will give, how we will decide, what we do when we disagree. A collaboration contract is a short, structured conversation that surfaces those things early — and writes them down so the group can hold itself to them.

This tool follows the five reflective questions of Holgaard et al. (2021) and maps them onto the Team Canvas, a one-page picture of a team. You move through the questions together; the app assembles your answers into the canvas below, which becomes your contract.
